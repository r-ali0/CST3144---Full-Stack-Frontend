About Us

Welcome to our Activity Booking platform! We would like to provide the best experience for our users! This platform allows parents who book after school classes for their children or students who book after school classes for themselves to purchase and manage classes online. This website is designed to be user friendly.

There are plently of classes to choose from. From Art, Computer Science, Triple Sciences, and more. Scroll down to see the classes we offer!
